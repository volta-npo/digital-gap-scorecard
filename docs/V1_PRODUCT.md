# Digital Gap Scorecard v1.0 Product Specification

## Workbench

Digital Equity Prioritization Desk

## Domain table

Applicant scorecards

## Metrics

- Need Score
- Feasibility Score
- Impact Potential

## Domain rows

- Business intake complete
- Evidence guides answered
- Need scored
- Feasibility scored
- Impact scored
- Student-fit scored
- Bias/conflict check complete
- Final ranking generated

## Generated deliverables

- Ranked applicant CSV
- Reviewer packet
- Anonymized sponsor summary

## Validation gates

- Evidence required for scores
- Bias check required
- Weights must sum to 100
